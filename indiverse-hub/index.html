Players · Discover & Follow Developers · Publish & Reach
01The Story

Built at Tuwaiq, for Saudi indie games.

Turki Mohammed Turki and Faisal Al Anazi met during Tuwaiq Academy's Flutter & Dart bootcamp. INDIVERSE is their final project — an answer to a gap they kept running into: Saudi indie games exist, but there's no single place to find them, follow their progress, or hear directly from the people building them.

INDIVERSE is that place. One app, two sides — players discover and follow, developers publish and connect.
